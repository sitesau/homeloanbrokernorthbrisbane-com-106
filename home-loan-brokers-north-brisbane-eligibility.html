Home Loan Brokers North Brisbane Eligibility Explained

Lenders rarely publish a single eligibility score. Instead, each institution weighs three inputs. Serviceability compares assessable income against credit cards, car loans, personal loans, dependants and living costs. Deposit position sets the loan-to-value ratio, which decides whether lenders mortgage insurance applies. Documentation confirms both, because overtime, bonuses and business income are often treated differently between lenders.

Because policy varies across major banks, second-tier lenders, mutuals and non-bank lenders, the same borrower can receive different answers from different institutions. That gap is where a broker comparison earns its keep.

How Serviceability Is Actually Calculated

Serviceability is not a simple income-minus-expenses sum. Lenders apply their own buffers and shading rules:

  • Income shading: overtime, bonuses and business income may be counted at less than their full face value.
  • Commitment loading: credit card limits, not just balances, are often treated as if fully drawn.
  • Household costs: dependants and living expenses are benchmarked, not taken on trust.
  • Rate buffers: repayments are tested at a rate above the quoted rate to allow for future rises.

A borrowing power calculator is a screening estimate only. A reliable figure needs a live serviceability assessment, which is the purpose of the free 30-minute review.

Deposit Position, LVR and Lenders Mortgage Insurance

The deposit decides the loan-to-value ratio, and the ratio decides cost. A smaller deposit can still be workable, but it may bring lenders mortgage insurance into play, adding a premium that is usually capitalised onto the loan.

  • Savings and equity: genuine savings histories matter to some lenders more than others.
  • Gift or guarantor support: a family gift or guarantee can change the LVR tier and the insurance outcome.
  • Grant discussion: first home buyers should test the First Home Owner Grant and the federal First Home Guarantee, which can alter the deposit maths.

The practical step is to ask which LVR tier each shortlisted lender applies and whether any policy excludes the borrower's deposit source entirely.

Documents That Confirm Eligibility Early

Most eligibility surprises trace back to paperwork arriving late. Preparing these items before the first conversation keeps the assessment honest:

  1. Recent payslips, tax returns or business financials matching the income type claimed.
  2. Statements for credit cards, car loans and personal loans, with current limits visible.
  3. Evidence of deposit: savings history, gift letter, equity estimate or grant eligibility notes.
  4. Contract details if a purchase is underway, including the finance clause date.
  5. For construction, the building contract and progress payment schedule.

A complete file lets the broker flag approval, valuation or timing issues before lodgement rather than after.

Timing Windows That Affect an Eligible Borrower

Being eligible on paper is not the same as settling on time. Two planning windows matter for North Brisbane files:

  • Purchase: allow 30 to 45 days from accepted contract to settlement on a standard purchase.
  • Refinance: expect 4 to 8 weeks, with the outgoing lender's discharge process the main variable.

These windows sit alongside the contract, conveyancer timetable and lender requirements, so the finance clause and settlement date should be checked against them before exchange. Common questions

Can a borrower be eligible with one lender but not another? Yes. Each institution applies its own serviceability shading, LVR tiers and deposit-source rules, so the same file can pass at one lender and fall short at another. Comparing across 30 or more lenders is how a broker surfaces that difference before lodgement.

Does a small deposit make a borrower ineligible? Not necessarily. A lower deposit raises the loan-to-value ratio and may trigger lenders mortgage insurance, but grant support, a family gift or a guarantor can change the position. First home buyers should also test the First Home Owner Grant and the federal First Home Guarantee.

How long does an eligible borrower need to allow for settlement? A standard purchase commonly allows 30 to 45 days from accepted contract to settlement. A refinance usually takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on the outgoing lender's discharge process, so discharge timing should be checked early.
